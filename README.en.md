# Awesome GPT-6 Astra

[中文](README.md) · English

A curated catalog of GPT-6 Astra cases with original links and author credit. This is an edited guide, not a dump, and not an official OpenAI repo.

GPT-6 Astra launched on 2026-09-03 and rolled out to Plus / Pro / Enterprise users on September 4–5. The interesting work on X was not the scoreboard. It was what people actually made after handing the computer to the model.

**Start here:** Anshu · Tom Krcha · Peter Gostev · Pietro · Guizang

The full writeups live in the [Chinese README](README.md), which is the default homepage. This page is the English mirror of the same 16 featured cases and the catalog.

## How to reuse the methods

Generate reference images first. Connect Blender or Ableton over MCP. Try Medium before Max. Host playable pages with ChatGPT Sites. Give the model a goal instead of step-by-step instructions. Minimize account permissions.

## Limits

Plus quotas burn fast. One-shot does not mean production-ready. Topology, housing details, and PCB routing still fail engineering checks. This catalog does not guarantee reproduction.
